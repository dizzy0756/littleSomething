The practical bits

Good questions.

How long does it take to make one?+

Most LittleSomethings are ready in under ten minutes. You can keep it beautifully simple or take your time making it extra personal.

Do either of us need an account?+

Nope. You make your surprise, and your person simply opens the private link — no login, app or download needed.

Who can open the link?+

Anyone you choose to share it with. The final link is private, so treat it like a small digital gift.

How does payment work?+

We use Razorpay for a secure ₹99 payment. Once your payment is confirmed, your private link is generated instantly and can be shared with your special someone.

Can I add photos and music?+

Yes — the builder lets you add a final photo and pick a song. Everything is currently a browser-only preview.

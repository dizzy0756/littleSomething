Plainly put

Privacy, plainly put.

Last updated: 19 August 2026. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account email: the email you use to sign in and to which we (or you) send the surprise link.
  • Your surprise content: the words, photos, song choice and details you add to build your LittleSomething.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive confirmation of payment but never store your card or bank details.

How we use it

We use your email to create your account, deliver your link, and provide support. Your surprise content is used only to generate and display the link you requested. We don’t sell your personal information.

Local storage

LittleSomething uses your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to save drafts of your surprise as you build it. This stays on your device; you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Third parties

We share only what’s needed with service providers: Razorpay (payments) and our hosting/CDN provider (to serve your surprise). They may process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and surprise content until you delete your account or ask us to remove it. Payment records are kept as required by Razorpay and applicable law.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us through our contact page and we’ll act on your request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here with a new date. Significant changes will be flagged through the Service.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, reach us via our contact page.

Cancellation & refunds.

LittleSomething is a one-time digital purchase — you pay ₹99 to generate a private surprise link. Because each link is a personalised digital product, the policy below explains when a refund is possible.

When you can get a refund

You can request a full refund if both of these are true:

  • Your request reaches us within 24 hours of the purchase; and
  • The surprise link has not yet been opened or shared with the recipient.

When refunds don’t apply

We’re unable to refund a purchase once the link has been opened or shared, or once 24 hours have passed since payment — the product has effectively been delivered and experienced.

How to request one

Send your request through our contact form, including the email address used at checkout and your order details. We’ll confirm eligibility and process approved refunds to your original payment method (Razorpay).

Processing time

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

Questions

Anything unclear? Reach us anytime via the contact form.
